What the benchmark measures

Frozen answer keys are written by a human labeller reading the live pages before seeing pipeline output. Verdicts on ambiguous cells are the labeller's and are bound to the exact shipped value judged — if a later run ships something else, the cell returns to CHECK rather than inheriting an old ruling. That binding is why VUM's tail cells were pinned to deterministic anchors: the tail re-phrased on every run, and one cell re-opened CHECK over a single semicolon. Key amendments happen only with a recorded, labeller-confirmed reason (3 to date). What sampled grading cannot do is police attribution — 18 NBU cells graded correct while shipping half a claim — which is the standing third review axis's job (docs/agents/attribution-review.md).
